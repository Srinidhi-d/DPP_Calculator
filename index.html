Round-trip latency

CS-RS scheme, per peer

Best RTT = 2 × one-way burst time + processing delay, plus the MAC ACK burst and inter-burst gap when MAC ACK is enabled. Transport-layer (TCP) ACKs are not part of this figure — they affect packet rate, not burst timing. PHS = payload header suppression (32 bytes suppressed). Two distinct acknowledgment mechanisms are modelled: the MAC ACK is a link-layer burst costing airtime (ms) and affects latency; the TCP ACK is a 40-byte transport packet costing bits and affects packet rate only. Traffic-interval figures include the ARP packet-duration term. TCP packets/min assume 40-byte TCP ACKs sharing bandwidth.
